iOS apps by Luke Bradford


Hi, I’m Luke. I’ve been building apps for iPhone and iPad since 2009. Every project is a labor of love, and most are free or free to try.



Symmetry Lab


A kaleidoscopic drawing app: create beautiful symmetrical art with just your finger. A few strokes and a strict rule of symmetry become an intricate pattern — no artistic skill required. Launched in 2009 and completely redesigned in 2026.


Free to download, with a subscription for the full version. More at symmetrylab.app.




Quarc


The classic falling-block puzzle, reimagined with curved pieces: every shape is built from concave and convex arcs, from circles to four-pointed stars.


Free to play forever, with a one-time $3.99 unlock for Sprint mode, Challenge mode, backgrounds, and themes. No subscriptions, no ads, no loot boxes — ever. More at the Quarc site.




Megalith


Turns a handful of words into a striking, block-justified text poster — the look of vintage justified typography, set in seconds. Free.




Textile


Textile is the iPhone notes app for people who just want an iPhone notes app. No login. No cloud accounts to manage. No half-built checklists. Just elegant typography and simple, rock-solid functionality.
