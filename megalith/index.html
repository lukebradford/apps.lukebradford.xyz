About

Megalith turns a handful of words into a striking, block-justified text graphic that you can shuffle, share, and set in motion. Every line is stretched to fill the same width, with each line’s font size adjusted to match, so your words form a solid block with clean edges on both sides. It’s the look of vintage justified posters, rebuilt as a fast, tactile app.

Features

  • Type a short phrase, and press return for an explicit line break
  • Shuffle fonts and layout with a single tap and unlimited undo
  • Choose a canvas shape: Square, Portrait, Story, or Poster
  • Set the text color and a background color or photo, with optional blur
  • Animate the result with smooth, dynamic letter-by-letter motion
  • Share the result or save it to your photo library

Privacy

Megalith collects no personal data and does no tracking. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs — everything happens on your device. Photos you choose as backgrounds are processed locally and never uploaded, and the app only writes to your photo library when you save a poster you’ve made.
