About

QUARC reimagines the classic falling block puzzle game with beautiful curved pieces. Every shape is built from concave and convex arcs. Rotate, drop, and clear rows to climb the leaderboard.

How to Play

  • Tap the left or right side of the screen to move a piece
  • Tap the center of the screen to rotate a piece
  • Swipe down to make a piece fall faster
  • Match curves to complete rows without gaps to clear them
  • Use golden pieces to fill unwanted gaps
  • Use a bomb to destroy an unwanted piece in midair

Privacy

QUARC uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as app launches, session duration, and gameplay events. This data helps me improve the game and is processed by Google in accordance with Firebase's Privacy Policy. No personally identifiable information is collected by QUARC itself.

QUARC also uses Game Center for leaderboards, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
