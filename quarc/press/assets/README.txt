THE PITCH
---------
QUARC reimagines the falling-block puzzle with one disarming twist: the
pieces are curved. Every shape is built from concave and convex arcs —
perfect circles, four-pointed stars, and everything between — and they
nestle into one another like tiles in a mosaic. Match the curves to seal a
row and clear it; misjudge the fit and a gap slips through. Beneath the
minimalist black-and-neon surface is a tense game of geometry and timing,
with multiple modes, swappable color themes, and Game Center leaderboards
to climb. It's an idea that feels instantly familiar, yet plays like
nothing you've touched before.
