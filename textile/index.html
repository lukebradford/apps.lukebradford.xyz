Textile

A distraction-free notepad for iOS

About

Textile is the iPhone notes app for people who just want an iPhone notes app. No login, no cloud accounts to manage, no half-built checklists. Just elegant typography and simple, rock-solid functionality.

Support

If you run into a bug, have a feature request, or just want to share feedback, get in touch and I’ll get back to you as soon as I can.

Contact support

Privacy

Textile does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Your notes live on your device and are never sent anywhere by Textile.

There are no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
