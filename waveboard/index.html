Honest about what it is

So you know before you download.

  • iPad only. It’s a canvas-and-cables interface and it wants the screen.
  • A standalone instrument, not an AUv3 plugin. It doesn’t load inside AUM, Loopy Pro or Cubasis. MIDI keyboards (USB or Bluetooth) work.
  • No Ableton Link. Think sketchpad and toy box, not a synced member of a live rig.
  • The microphone is only listened to. A few instruments follow how loud you are. Nothing from the mic is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Yours. No account, no login, no ads, no tracking. Instruments are small files in the Files app.
